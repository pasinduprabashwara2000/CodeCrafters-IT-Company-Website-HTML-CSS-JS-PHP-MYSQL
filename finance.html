FAQ

Questions We Hear From Fintech Teams

Common questions about building financial software with us.

How do you handle regulatory compliance?

We build KYC/AML, audit logging and data-handling requirements into the architecture from day one, and work with your compliance or legal team to align with the specific regulations that apply in your jurisdiction.

Can you integrate with our existing banking or payment providers?

Yes. We regularly integrate with card processors, open banking APIs, core banking systems and payment rails, and can work within the constraints of providers you already use.

How do you keep financial data secure?

Encryption in transit and at rest, strict access controls, tokenized card data and independent security review are standard on every financial project, not an add-on.

How long does a fintech platform take to build?

A focused MVP typically takes 6 to 10 weeks. A full digital banking platform or custom trading system usually runs 4 to 9 months depending on integrations and compliance scope.

Do you support the platform after launch?

Yes. Every package includes a post-launch support window, and we offer ongoing maintenance, monitoring and security patching plans for production financial systems.
