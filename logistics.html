FAQ

Questions We Hear From Logistics Teams

Common questions about building fleet, warehouse and delivery software with us.

Can drivers use the app in areas with poor signal?

Yes. The driver app is designed to keep working offline, cache updates locally and sync automatically once a connection is available again.

Can you integrate with our existing fleet or warehouse hardware?

In most cases, yes. We can work with existing GPS trackers, barcode scanners and ERP systems rather than requiring a full hardware replacement.

How accurate is the route optimization?

Routes factor in live traffic, stop windows and vehicle capacity, and we tune the model against your actual delivery data rather than shipping a generic default.

Can customers track their own deliveries?

Yes. Customer-facing tracking pages and SMS or push notifications are part of the core platform, with live ETA updates as the shipment moves.

Do you support the platform after launch?

Yes. Every package includes a post-launch support window, and we offer ongoing maintenance plans that cover new routes, fleet growth and technical support.
