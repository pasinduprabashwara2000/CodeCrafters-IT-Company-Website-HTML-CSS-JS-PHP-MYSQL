FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often about website design and development projects.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most starter and business websites take between 2 and 6 weeks depending on complexity, content readiness and the number of revision rounds. Custom web applications and e-commerce platforms typically take longer.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can review your current website and either redesign it from scratch or improve specific pages, performance and structure while keeping the content you want to retain.

What do I need to provide before we start?

Helpful things include your logo and brand assets, any existing content or copy, examples of websites you like, and a general idea of your target pages and features. We can also help fill in the gaps during discovery.

Do you provide hosting and domain setup?

We can guide you through choosing and configuring hosting and a domain, or work directly with a hosting provider and domain registrar of your choice.

Do you offer support after the website launches?

Yes. Every package includes an initial support window after launch, and ongoing website maintenance plans are available for updates, monitoring and technical support.
