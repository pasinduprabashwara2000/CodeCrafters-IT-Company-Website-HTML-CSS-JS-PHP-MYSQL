Yes. Websites depend on software, hosting, integrations, content and security components that may require updates over time.

Can you maintain a website built by another company?

Yes. We can review the existing website and technology stack first, then determine the maintenance work required.

Can I request content changes?

Content and website updates can be included depending on your selected maintenance plan and the scope of the request.

Do you support e-commerce websites?

Yes. Our Advanced Maintenance plan is designed for e-commerce websites and custom web applications.
