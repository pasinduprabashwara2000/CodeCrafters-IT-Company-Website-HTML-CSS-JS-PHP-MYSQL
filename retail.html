FAQ

Questions We Hear From Retailers

Common questions about building retail and e-commerce software with us.

Can the platform handle sale-day traffic spikes?

Yes. We design and load-test for peak events like seasonal sales, so checkout stays fast and stock stays accurate even when traffic jumps well above normal.

Can you sync inventory between our website and physical stores?

Yes. Real-time inventory sync across your online store, POS and warehouses is a core part of the omnichannel package, so stock levels stay accurate everywhere.

Which payment methods can you support?

We integrate cards, digital wallets, buy-now-pay-later and cash-on-delivery depending on your market, all routed through a single checkout flow.

Can you migrate our existing store and product data?

Yes. We can migrate products, customers and order history from most existing platforms with minimal downtime during the switch.

Do you support the platform after launch?

Yes. Every package includes a post-launch support window, and we offer ongoing maintenance plans that cover new features, seasonal readiness checks and support.
