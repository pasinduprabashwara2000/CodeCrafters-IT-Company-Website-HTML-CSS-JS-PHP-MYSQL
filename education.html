FAQ

Questions We Hear From Educators

Common questions about building learning software with us.

Will this work for students with unreliable internet?

Yes. We design lessons and quizzes to cache locally and sync once a connection returns, and keep pages light so they load reasonably on slower networks and older devices.

Can you integrate with our existing student information system?

In most cases, yes. We can connect to existing SIS, SSO and payment providers so you are not forced to migrate everything at once.

How do you protect student data?

Student records are handled with strict access controls and data-minimization practices aligned with COPPA/FERPA-style expectations, and we scope exactly what each role can see.

Can teachers manage content without our help?

Yes. Course authoring, grading and reporting are built for non-technical staff, so your team can update content and manage classes without filing a support ticket.

Do you support the platform after launch?

Yes. Every package includes a post-launch support window, and we offer ongoing maintenance plans that cover new terms, feature requests and technical support.
