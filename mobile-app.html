FAQ

Common Mobile App Questions

Should I build native or cross-platform?

Native (Swift or Kotlin) suits apps that need maximum performance or deep platform-specific features. Flutter or React Native suits most business apps and reduces cost and timeline by sharing one codebase.

How long does it take to build an app?

A focused single-platform app typically takes 6 to 10 weeks. Cross-platform and enterprise apps usually take longer depending on features and integrations.

Do you handle App Store and Play Store submission?

Yes. We manage account setup guidance, store listings, review requirements and the submission process for both platforms.

Can you add a backend to my app?

Yes. We build secure REST APIs, authentication, databases and third-party integrations such as payments, maps and chat.

Do you support existing apps?

Yes. We can take over an existing codebase, fix bugs, add features or modernize an outdated app.

Is support available after launch?

Every plan includes an initial support window, and ongoing maintenance plans are available for updates, monitoring and technical support.
